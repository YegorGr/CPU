IN
POPR AX
PUSHR AX
PUSH 0
JB negative

CALL fact 
PUSHR BX
OUT
HLT

fact:
    PUSHR AX
    PUSH 1
    JA recurse
    PUSH 1
    POPR BX
    RET

recurse:
    PUSHR AX
    PUSHR AX
    PUSH 1
    SUB
    POPR AX
    CALL fact
    PUSHR BX
    MUL
    POPR BX
    RET

negative:
    PUSH -1
    OUT
    HLT
